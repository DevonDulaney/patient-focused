What We Ask of You
One report. Already in your system.

Your office manager runs one report from your scheduling system — procedures scheduled, cancellations, and no-shows over the last 12 months. Aggregate totals only: no patient information, and no access to your system. The data is already there; we just analyze it.

We handle everything else — we compile the data, run the analysis, build the peer comparison, and deliver the readout. If your system can’t produce a clean report, we’ll set up a simple 30-day tracking sheet instead. No obligation, either way.

Common Questions
How much time does this actually take?
Almost none on your end. Your office manager runs one report from your scheduling system — scheduled procedures, cancellations, and no-shows over the last 12 months — and sends us the totals. About fifteen minutes, and no patient data. If your system can’t pull it cleanly, we set up a simple tracking sheet instead.
What happens after you have the data?
We schedule a 30-minute readout where we walk through your numbers, the peer benchmark, and the research. No obligation. If the data reveals an opportunity, we can discuss what a solution looks like. If it doesn’t, you still have your data.
What do you do with my data?
Your individual data is yours. In any published findings, all practice data is anonymized — no names, no identifying details.
Who is behind this?
Patient Focused is a healthcare content studio that builds physician-on-camera education libraries for private procedural practices. This diagnostic is how we learn your practice — and how you learn your numbers.
