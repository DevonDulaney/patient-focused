About

Five years of medical storytelling. One mission.

Patient Focused is built by someone who’s spent half a decade in the room where physicians meet patients on camera.

Patient Focused

Patient Focused was founded by a content producer and editor based in South Florida who spent five years working on an Emmy Award-winning medical documentary series — producing and editing over 100 patient stories. The series airs on over 80 public television stations nationally and on Roku.

That work taught us how physicians explain things to patients, what language builds trust, and what makes someone feel informed versus overwhelmed. Patient Focused applies that knowledge to private practice: physician-on-camera education libraries built around the questions your patients are actually asking.

The world-class private practice is built on the relationship between patient and provider. The relationship doesn’t end when the visit does.
