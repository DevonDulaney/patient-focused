Pre-procedure video education delivered via text cut non-completion from 37.6% to 13.2% across 14 private practices. At $5,000 per cancelled case, the math is immediate.

Ballon-Landa E, et al. Am J Mens Health. 2019;13(6):1557988319892738.

Folcarelli P. “How a Robust Informed Consent Process Enhances Patient Safety.” Candello Blog, Nov 16, 2023. Data: Adam Schaffer, MD, MPH, Candello Annual Community Summit, Sept 12, 2023.
